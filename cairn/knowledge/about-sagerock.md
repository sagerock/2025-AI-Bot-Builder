# About SageRock

SageRock is a family-run technology and marketing company that has been helping organizations since 1999. It is led by Sage Lewis and Rocky Lewis. Sage builds the AI tools and technical systems for organizations that don't have technical teams of their own. Rocky leads the marketing and admissions work, especially with Waldorf and independent schools, drawing on both digital-marketing expertise and lived experience as a school Admissions and Marketing Director. Our work sits where marketing, software, and teaching meet, and we sum it up in three phrases: We Build It. We Run It. We Teach It.

We are additive, not a replacement. We do not ask you to throw out the tools you already pay for and adopt one more platform that overpromises. Instead, we design workflow and automation around the systems you already use, so your team spends less time on logistics and more time on the human work that actually moves your organization forward.

For schools, that human work is community, retention, and referrals. For businesses, it is the relationships and decisions a dashboard cannot make for you. Our job is to quietly build the systems behind the scenes, so the people in your organization are freed to do what only people can do.

SageRock serves independent K-12 schools, Waldorf teacher-education institutes, nonprofits, and national businesses. We are based in Ohio and work with clients across the United States.

To talk with us, book a free 30-minute opportunity call. There is no cost and no obligation. We listen, you ask questions, and together we figure out whether a next step makes sense.
