# How SageRock Works With Schools

SageRock works alongside schools to build marketing and admissions systems that reliably support enrollment and retention, so admissions teams have more time for the in-person work that builds community, referrals, and a vibrant school.

Our engagement follows four steps.

## 1. Explore: a free 30-minute consultation

We begin with a conversation about your school's admissions and marketing goals, your current processes, your challenges, and your opportunities. You leave with fresh ideas, practical suggestions, and a clearer sense of how SageRock can help. There is no cost and no obligation.

## 2. Analyze: a deep dive into your enrollment funnel

We review every stage of your marketing and admissions process, from inquiry generation to lead follow-through to enrollment and reenrollment. You receive a detailed audit with actionable recommendations that help you strengthen and streamline your funnel. This step is the Enrollment Audit.

## 3. Implement: turning strategy into systems

If you choose to move forward, we help build and implement the recommendations from your audit. Everything is tailored to your school's needs and budget, and designed to work with your current technology, staff capacity, and enrollment goals.

## 4. Support: training and a sustainable transition

For three months we work alongside your team to train, refine, and document the new processes. After that, you choose the level of ongoing support that fits, anything from full independence to regular consulting and management.

The point is never the technology. The point is fewer hours on follow-up emails and reminder spreadsheets, and more hours with the families, students, and teachers who chose your school.
