# SageRock AI Assistants: Many Sources In, One Assistant Out

Most organizations have their information scattered across a dozen systems: a CRM, an email tool, a scheduling app, ad accounts, web analytics, a student information system, and more. The data is all there, but no one has time to pull it together. SageRock's answer is a pattern we build for each client: many sources in, one assistant out.

## How it works

**Tier 1, your sources.** We connect to the systems you already use and sync from them on a schedule, some in real time and some nightly. Nothing changes in those systems.

**Tier 2, one system of record.** Everything flows into a single, unified data store that belongs to you. Each client's data is tenant-scoped and kept separate. The assistant reads only from this store.

**Tier 3, your assistant.** A named AI assistant, reachable by email, reads across all of your sources and answers questions or drafts replies in plain language.

## Built to be trustworthy

The assistants are deliberately read-only. They read across your data and draft answers, but they never write back to your source systems and never change your ad accounts, your records, or your inbox. A person always reviews and sends. There is no auto-send and no upstream writes. The assistant does the gathering and the first draft, and a human stays in control.
