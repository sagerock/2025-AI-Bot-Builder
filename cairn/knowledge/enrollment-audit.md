# The Enrollment Audit and Wire-Up

The Enrollment Audit is a deep dive into your school's marketing and enrollment funnel. We map your sources, walk the journey a family takes from their very first hello, and hand you a prioritized, plain-English plan. The audit is $2,500.

## What we examine

We look at your funnel one source at a time:

- **Inquiry forms on your website.** Where does a submission go, and how fast does a family hear back? We measure time-to-reply.
- **Tours and visits.** How do families book a tour, and how many tours become applications? We measure the tour-to-application rate.
- **Your admissions inbox.** Who watches it, and what is the real response time, including over the summer? We measure coverage and speed.
- **Email marketing.** What reaches families between inquiry and decision, and is it being opened? We look at lists and open rates.
- **Student records and applications.** Is your application data connected to your inquiry data, or living on a separate island? We trace inquiry through to enrolled.
- **Website visitor tracking.** Do you know who is on your site, and when a family comes back to look again?
- **Paid advertising.** What are you spending, and what does one enrolled family actually cost?
- **Web analytics.** Which channels drive your inquiries, and is anyone acting on what they show?

## What we produce

We build your family-journey map, end to end, so you can see how reaching out actually feels from a family's point of view.

## What you walk away with

A clear report with four things: a funnel map showing where families fall away, a picture of which of your sources are connected versus siloed versus missing, the family's-eye view of your process, and a prioritized plan that takes you from where you are today to a wired-up system with one place for your data and one assistant to ask.

The Enrollment Audit and Wire-Up includes the full funnel audit plus wiring up your tools so you are ready to go. Ongoing plans afterward start at $100 per month.
