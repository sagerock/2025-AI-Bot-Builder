# SageRock Pricing and Plans

SageRock keeps pricing simple and tailored to your needs.

**Free opportunity call.** A 30-minute consultation costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

**Enrollment Audit and Wire-Up: $2,500.** A full audit of your marketing and enrollment funnel, plus wiring up your tools so your data lands in one place. This is the usual starting point for schools.

**Ongoing plans start at $100 per month.** Once your systems are built, you choose the level of support that fits your school. Plans range from a lighter Report and Ask level up to fully Managed support, which starts at $250 per month. You can also stay fully independent after the three-month transition if you prefer.

Everything is scoped to your school's budget and staff capacity. If you are not sure where to start, book a free opportunity call and we will help you find the right fit.
