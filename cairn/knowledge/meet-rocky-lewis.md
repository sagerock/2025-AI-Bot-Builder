# Meet Rocky Lewis

Rocky Lewis co-leads SageRock alongside Sage Lewis, heading the company's work with Waldorf and independent schools on enrollment and marketing. You can reach her at rocky@sagerock.com or 330-321-3487.

Rocky is a writer by trade. She worked as an editor in publishing for many years, then carried those writing skills into digital marketing: content creation, SEO, social media, blogs, and paid search. That work grew into SageRock, where she has helped clients tell their stories for much of the company's 27-year history.

Her overarching strength is project planning, organization, and management. Over the years she has project-managed large digital marketing and social media campaigns for major national brands, and she brings deep hands-on skill across digital marketing, content creation, website management, email marketing, social media, graphic design, GA4, Google Ads, FACTS, Salesforce, and a range of CRMs.

In 2020 Rocky stepped fully into Waldorf education, serving as Admissions and Marketing Director at Spring Garden Waldorf School. There she led the kind of work she now helps other schools with: project-managing a six-week shift to online learning during COVID at a school that had almost no technology, moving that school from paper and an outdated student information system onto FACTS for records, evaluations, and admissions, and managing a seven-year accreditation process end to end, including compiling and writing the documentation.

After years of lived admissions experience from inside a Waldorf school, Rocky is returning to full-time work with SageRock. She brings both consulting expertise and real admissions experience to schools that want stronger systems, clearer processes, and sustainable enrollment. Her goal is simple: to help admissions and marketing colleagues spend less time on logistics and more time on the strategies that engage families and drive enrollment.
